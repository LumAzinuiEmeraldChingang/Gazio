About Us

  Our mission is to empower household managers, food service businesses—both small and large and gas cylinder vendors with practical, efficient, and user-friendly solutions.

Our Story

   Our journey began in a lecture hall, where we were grouped into teams of six and challenged to develop an innovative solution. Inspired by the everyday struggles of our people, we identified a common problem—finding a specific gas cylinder often requires spending hours under the scorching sun or in cold rain, while carrying heavy metal bottles in search of refills. This realization deeply moved us. Determined to make a difference, we decided to apply the skills we acquired at the ICT University of Yaoundé to develop a practical solution to this challenge.
Thus, GAZIO was born.

What We Do

   GAZIO acts as a bridge between gas cylinder vendors and buyers. Users can search or browse for a specific gas cylinder type and instantly receive a list of available vendors closest to their location. The platform allows users to reserve gas cylinders and contact vendors directly for additional information. GAZIO serves as a centralized digital marketplace, enabling households and businesses to easily find verified gas vendors and purchase gas efficiently, safely, and conveniently.
