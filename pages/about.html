About Gazio — Reliable, Local Gas Marketplace

We connect buyers and verified local gas vendors through a secure, easy-to-use platform — saving time, improving safety, and ensuring fair prices for every transaction.

Founded at the ICT University of Yaoundé, Gazio began as a student project and became a mission to simplify access to gas supplies for households and businesses. We focus on trust, convenience, and excellent customer support.

Fast & Convenient
